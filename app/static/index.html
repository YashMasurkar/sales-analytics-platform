About the Platform

Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence Engine designed for high data integrity.

1. Core Analytical Integrity Principles

Unlike superficial dashboards that silently impute zeros or fabricate missing values, this system implements rigorous data auditing:

  • No Statistical Imputation: Missing numerical values (revenue, cost, quantity, dates) are detected, audited, and excluded with explicit reasoning rather than statistically invented. Missing categorical dimensions are clearly labeled with an explicit 'Unspecified' placeholder.
  • Returns & Adjustments Preserved: Negative quantities and revenues are classified as anomalies for audit transparency, not silently deleted.
  • Explicit Derivations: Derived metrics (e.g. Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice × (1 - Discount)) are tracked and audited.
  • Conservative Schema Mapping: Unrestricted fuzzy matching is avoided to prevent ambiguous field mappings.
